## Ідея

My goal is to build a high-performance 5-inch freestyle FPV drone from scratch. I'm 14 years old and this is my first major hardware project where I'll be learning advanced soldering, electronics, and radio protocols.

_З README.md, без переказу._

## Для чого

I will build a fully functional FPV drone using the DJI O3 Air Unit. I will solder and assemble all parts myself, design and 3D print custom components, configure the flight controller, and document the entire process with photos and videos. This project will teach me electronics, drone assembly, digital FPV systems, and 3D design.
