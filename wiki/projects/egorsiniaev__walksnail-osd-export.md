## Ідея

Export **Walksnail Avatar** goggle `.osd` recordings to plain text for analysis.

Inspired by [pymsposd](https://github.com/sbvualo/pymsposd), which does the same job for the msp-osd / DJI format. This tool reuses its idea of extracting telemetry by locating OSD glyphs, adapted to the Walksnail Avatar container.

_З README.md, без переказу._

## Для чого

Export Walksnail Avatar (.osd) goggle recordings to text and labelled telemetry CSV. Inspired by pymsposd.
