    Suggested ranges for what's unset

      Estimates use simplified physics (static-thrust and momentum-theory models). By default they're computed at full-charge voltage assuming props load motors to ~75% of their unloaded RPM ceiling — both adjustable under Advanced, along with altitude, battery chemistry, and a thrust-stand calibration point. Expect ±20–30% versus a bench unless calibrated — use these numbers to compare options and catch mismatches, not to size fuses.
